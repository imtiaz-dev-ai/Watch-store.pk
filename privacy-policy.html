Information We Collect

We collect only what is needed to serve you: your name, phone number, email, delivery address and order history. Payment details for COD orders are never stored on our servers.

How We Use Your Information

  • Processing and delivering your orders
  • Sending order confirmations and delivery updates
  • Providing customer support and warranty service
  • Sharing offers and new arrivals — only if you subscribe to our newsletter

Cookies

Our website uses minimal browser storage (localStorage) to remember your cart, wishlist and login session on your own device. This data never leaves your browser and can be cleared anytime from your browser settings.

Data Sharing

We share your delivery details with our courier partners strictly to complete your order. We never sell, rent or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing purposes.

Data Security

Access to customer data is restricted to authorised staff, and all communication with our support channels is handled over secured systems.

Your Rights

You may request a copy, correction or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing info@watchstore.pk. We respond to all privacy requests within 7 working days.
