Delivery Coverage

We deliver to every city, town and village in Pakistan through our courier partners (TCS, Leopards & M&P). Whether you are in Karachi, Islamabad, Peshawar or a remote area, your watch will reach you.

Delivery Timelines

  • Lahore: 1–2 working days
  • Major cities (Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan): 2–3 working days
  • Other areas: 3–4 working days

Orders placed before 3:00 PM are dispatched the same day; orders on weekends and public holidays are dispatched on the next working day.

Shipping Charges

Shipping is completely free on all orders, all over Pakistan — no minimum order value. The price you see at checkout is the final price you pay.

Order Tracking

As soon as your parcel is handed to the courier, we SMS and email you a tracking number. You can follow your parcel in real time, or call our 24/7 helpline at +92 300 1234567 and we will track it for you.

Delays & Undeliverable Parcels

Rare carrier delays caused by weather or holidays may extend delivery by 1–2 days. If a parcel cannot be delivered after three attempts, it returns to us and we will contact you to arrange redelivery at no extra cost.
