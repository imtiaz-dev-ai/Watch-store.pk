7-Day Return Window

If you are not completely satisfied, you may request a return or exchange within 7 days of delivery. Simply contact us at info@watchstore.pk or +92 300 1234567 with your order number.

Eligibility Conditions

  • The watch is unused, unworn and in the same condition as delivered
  • Original box, tags, manuals, warranty card and all accessories are included
  • Proof of purchase (order number or invoice) is provided

Non-Returnable Items

Watches with engraved or personalised case-backs and items marked as final sale cannot be returned, but they remain covered by the 1-year store warranty.

How to Return

  • Step 1 — Contact our support team with your order number and reason
  • Step 2 — We arrange free courier pickup from your doorstep
  • Step 3 — Our quality team inspects the watch within 48 hours of receipt
  • Step 4 — Refund or exchange is processed immediately after approval

Refund Timeline

Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method (or as bank/Easypaisa transfer) within 5–7 working days. COD orders are refunded via bank transfer or mobile wallet after verification.

Warranty Claims

Manufacturing defects are covered by a 1-year store warranty. Warranty claims are handled separately from returns — we repair or replace the watch free of charge, including two-way shipping.
